### 👋 About me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Intelligence Science and Technology at Peking University. I will join the School of Intelligence at Peking University as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2027.

My research interests lie broadly in foundation models, with a particular focus on multimodal large language models, diffusion language models, multimodal generation and understanding, intelligent agents, and efficient model training and inference.
