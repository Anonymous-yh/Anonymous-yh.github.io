### 👋 About me

I am a senior undergraduate student majoring in Intelligence Science and Technology at Peking University (PKU). Since February 2025, I have been a research intern at [MSALab](https://msalab-pku.github.io/), Peking University.

In September 2027, I will join the School of Intelligence Science and Technology (SIST) at Peking University as a Ph.D. student.

My research interests lie broadly in foundation models, with a particular focus on **multimodal large language models, diffusion language models, and multimodal generation and understanding**.
