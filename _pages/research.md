## 🔭 Research

I am interested in building more capable and efficient foundation models. My current interests include the following directions.

<div class="research-grid">
  <div class="research-card"><div class="research-body"><h4 class="research-title">Multimodal Large Language Models</h4><p class="research-desc">Models that connect language with visual and other modalities for grounded understanding and reasoning.</p></div></div>
  <div class="research-card"><div class="research-body"><h4 class="research-title">Diffusion Large Language Models</h4><p class="research-desc">Diffusion-based language modeling, including generation quality, inference and training methods.</p></div></div>
  <div class="research-card"><div class="research-body"><h4 class="research-title">Multimodal Generation</h4><p class="research-desc">Generative systems that can understand and produce rich multimodal content.</p></div></div>
  <div class="research-card"><div class="research-body"><h4 class="research-title">LLM and Multimodal Agents</h4><p class="research-desc">Agents that reason, use tools, and interact reliably with their environments.</p></div></div>
  <div class="research-card"><div class="research-body"><h4 class="research-title">Efficient Training and Inference</h4><p class="research-desc">Methods for making capable foundation models more practical to train and deploy.</p></div></div>
</div>
