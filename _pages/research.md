## 🔭 Research

I am interested in foundation models that connect language, perception, and generation. My current work and reading are organized around three closely related directions.

<div class="research-focus-list" markdown="0">
  <section class="research-focus">
    <span class="research-focus__index">01</span>
    <div>
      <p class="research-focus__label">Multimodal LLMs</p>
      <h2>Grounded understanding and reasoning across modalities.</h2>
      <p>I am interested in models that bring visual evidence, language, and structured reasoning together for reliable perception and interaction.</p>
      <p class="research-focus__questions">Questions: How can multimodal models represent fine-grained visual information? How can perception remain accurate when a task involves many regions or inputs?</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-focus">
    <span class="research-focus__index">02</span>
    <div>
      <p class="research-focus__label">Diffusion LMs</p>
      <h2>Generation beyond left-to-right decoding.</h2>
      <p>I study diffusion language models as an alternative generation paradigm, especially their parallel decoding behavior, few-step generation, and the training objectives that make those capabilities practical.</p>
      <p class="research-focus__questions">Questions: What should be distilled from a multi-step diffusion process? Which generation states and schedules support faster inference without sacrificing quality?</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="research-focus">
    <span class="research-focus__index">03</span>
    <div>
      <p class="research-focus__label">Multimodal Generation &amp; Understanding</p>
      <h2>Connecting perception with rich generative systems.</h2>
      <p>I am interested in the shared representations, objectives, and evaluation settings that let a model both understand multimodal input and generate useful multimodal output.</p>
      <p class="research-focus__questions">Questions: How can generation and understanding reinforce one another? How should multimodal capabilities be evaluated beyond a single benchmark or modality?</p>
    </div>
  </section>
</div>
